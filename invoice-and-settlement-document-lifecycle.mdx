---
title: "Invoice & Settlement Document Lifecycle"
---

The platform manages two parallel document streams:

| Stream | Document | Relationship |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Platform fees** | Invoice (`invoices` table) | Platform → <br />Instance operator |
| **Distribution** | Settlement Statement (`merchant_settlements`) | Instance (Issuer) → <br />Merchant |
| **Distribution** | Rebate Notice (`merchant_rebates`) | Instance (Issuer) → <br />Merchant |
