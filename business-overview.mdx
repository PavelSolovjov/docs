# What is Ton Whales

[TON Whales](https://tonwhales.com/staking) is a leading infrastructure provider for the [TON blockchain](https://ton.org/en), specializing in comprehensive staking solutions. The company acts as a reliable intermediary between users and the complex technical infrastructure of the blockchain, offering secure and convenient tools for delegating funds.\
The main objective of [TON Whales](https://tonwhales.com/staking) is to democratize access to TON staking — the company helps both individual users and institutional clients effectively participate in network support and earn stable income from staking, while completely relieving them of the need to understand the complex technical aspects of validator setup and infrastructure management.\
[The TON Whales platform](https://tonwhales.com/staking) offers diverse staking solutions adapted to various user needs: from basic pools for beginners to specialized solutions for large holders and institutional investors. All staking pools operate based on proven smart contracts that have undergone independent security audits.\
Currently (11/02/2026), the [TON Whales](https://tonwhales.com/staking) ecosystem includes 7 active staking pools with more than 53 million [TON](https://ton.org/en) locked from users worldwide. This makes [TON Whales](https://tonwhales.com/staking) one of the largest staking providers in the [TON](https://ton.org/en) ecosystem by assets under management.

# TON Whales Mission

TON Whales' mission is to become the world's largest staking provider in TON by volume of locked funds, providing users with the simplest and safest way to earn from staking and contribute to network stability.

# TON Whales Products

- **Staking pools** — decentralized smart contracts that combine funds from multiple users to participate in TON blockchain validation. Pools allow TON holders to receive staking rewards without the need to run their own validator and with minimal technical knowledge.
- **Monitoring system** — an open Grafana platform for real-time tracking of the status and performance of all TON Whales validators. The system ensures transparency of infrastructure operations and allows users to monitor the security of their staked funds.
- **Validators** — specialized servers that process transactions, create new blocks, and maintain TON network security. TON Whales validators provide the technical infrastructure for staking pool operations and reward distribution to participants.
- **Staking smart contract** — a decentralized program on the [TON blockchain](https://ton.org/en) that automatically manages user token locking for network validation participation and distributes rewards proportionally to each participant's contribution size.
- Website [**tonwhales.com**](http://tonwhales.com) - web interface for TON staking management, through which users can select pools, track profitability, and manage delegated funds.

# Integrations

TON Whales staking solutions are currently available to users through several integrated platforms and services:

- Mobile wallet [**Tonhub**](https://tonhub.com/) — TON Whales' proprietary non-custodial solution with built-in staking functionality
- Mobile wallet [**Tonkeeper**](https://tonkeeper.com/) — popular TON ecosystem wallet with integrated staking pools
- Hardware wallet [**Ledger**](https://www.ledger.com/) — cold storage with secure staking capability through hardware device
- Staking provider platform [**P2P.org**](http://P2P.org) — international institutional staking service with TON Whales pool support

# History

- **October 2021** — launch of the first validator for staking own mined coins
- **March 14, 2022** — smart contract presented and first decentralized staking pool [Nominators](https://tonwhales.com/staking/pool/nominators) launched, available through [tonwhales.com](http://tonwhales.com). In the first week, the pool accumulated 7.5 million TON, the contract passed the first informal audit by the TON Foundation team
- **April 2022** — integration of TON Whales staking into proprietary non-custodial Tonhub wallet
- **August 2022** — launch of second staking pool [Whales Club](https://tonwhales.com/staking/pool/club) for holders of the eponymous NFT collection
- **September 2022** — introduction of third pool [Whales team](https://tonwhales.com/staking/pool/team) for largest NFT holders and team members
- **November 2022** — launch of [open Grafana monitoring](https://mon.whalescorp.com/public-dashboards/443fd6e0479d4d9b8f05ec87d92f9c2f) for tracking all TON Whales validators status and liquid pool [Whales Liquid](https://tonwhales.com/staking/liquid)
- **February 2023** — implementation of proprietary DAO contract
- **May 2023** — launch of first partnership pools with [EPN](https://epn.bz/en) and [Tonkeeper](https://tonkeeper.com/) teams. Total staking balance exceeded 25 million TON
- **June 2024** — return to staking development after working on Holders project, [opening of first celebrity staking pool](https://t.me/c/1752529545/108214/130613) in collaboration with rapper Morgenshtern
- **September 2025** — strategic partnership agreements with [P2P.org](http://P2P.org) and [Ledger](https://www.ledger.com/). Launch of [joint staking pools](https://tonwhales.com/staking/pool/p2p)

**Present time** — TON Whales total staking balance exceeded 50 million TON, making it one of the largest in the ecosystem
