# What is Holders

Holders SDK is a B2B platform (SDK) that allows partners (wallets, banks, fintechs) to quickly and easily integrate crypto-fiat payment functions for secure monetization of their audience without radical product transformation.\
Holders provides clear documentation, expertise in traffic and security (including anti-fraud and reputation protection), as well as full integration support. Holders SDK helps partners increase revenue, improve their service valuation, and gives their users the ability to spend all their money through the partner's application.

# Holders for Users

Holders is a solution that provides the simplest and most convenient use of stablecoins for making payments, transfers, and opening vestings directly in the user's mobile wallet through cards and accounts while maintaining full control over assets and private keys.

# **Holders SDK Mission**

The key goal of Holders SDK is to give partners the opportunity to **monetize their existing audience** by integrating fintech functions **without the need for radical restructuring** of their core product. We strive to **increase equity (capitalization) and revenue** of partner products, increasing their efficiency and allowing their users to **spend all money through the partner's service**, not just the funds already inside the service.

# **Holders SDK Vision**

1. Become the leading B2B solution for embedding non-custodial crypto-fiat payments.
2. Be not just a technology provider, but a **partner in integration and growth**, supported by a strong team in implementation, sales, and marketing support.
3. Provide partners with expertise and audit for effective implementation and **value delivery** of integrated solutions to their end users.

# Holders Philosophy

In ancient times, money could only be physical: hides, coins, paper. Everyone who had them owned them personally. People always wondered: how to preserve them? How to prevent them from being stolen, taken away, or lost in a fire? Funds were stored in chests and treasures, but more creative options were also invented: in beehives and fish carcasses, in coffins, in vessels with holy water. However, using money hidden in a fish carcass is inconvenient, and a chest can be broken into or carried away, and the money will still be lost.\
Everything changed with the emergence of banks. No longer need to hide money, it can be stored in a bank, which takes responsibility for storage. Robbers began to carry away safes less often, and everyone has long forgotten about storing funds in chests. Bank cards allowed people to forget what cash looks like, and smartphone payments eliminated the need to use the cards themselves.\
But with increased security and convenience, a new problem emerged: **people no longer own their money, now the Bank owns it.** The bank decides how much you can spend, where and to whom you can transfer. The bank can freeze funds at any moment and decide for itself whether to return them. The bank will independently transfer information about your balances, expenses, and deposits to tax authorities.\
But how not to lose all the conveniences that the bank provides, while maintaining personal ownership of funds? Holders created a unique solution that allows both maintaining full personal control over money and using all modern conveniences of the banking system - non-custodial accounts based on smart contracts.

# Architecture and Principles

Holders was built as a unified ecosystem — from storage and investments to payments and p2p transfers. All ecosystem products are based on unified principles:

- **Non-custodial nature**: users retain ownership of their own keys and funds
- **Simplicity over complex technologies**: complex technical infrastructure is hidden behind intuitive interfaces
- **Unified approach to user experience**: common interface patterns, terminology, and visual language
- **Unified approach to security**: common standards for key management and transaction monitoring

# **Holders SDK Target Audience**

Businesses and applications with existing audiences seeking to integrate financial services and monetization: crypto wallets, banks (traditional and neo), content platforms (e.g., [Fragment](https://fragment.com)), fintech companies, Forex brokers, gaming platforms, and other services working with digital values.

# History

**2023**

- **Project launch (January 2023).** Creation of a B2B platform for payments between cryptocurrencies and regular money. Search for partners among financial companies.
- Submission of documents to [VISA](https://visa.com) for working with cryptocurrencies.

**2024**

- Interface simplification for regular users
- Release of the first prepaid card with partner [CardB](https://cardb.com).
- Obtaining permission from [VISA](https://visa.com) to work with cryptocurrencies.
- Launch of the first decentralized account.
- Successful testing in [Tonhub](https://tonhub.com). Users began using USDT accounts in Tonhub (a product based on Holders) instead of dollar accounts in banks, and wallets with Holders technology instead of digital banks.
- Testing international SEPA/SWIFT transfers with [Triple A](https://triple-a.io) company.
- Obtaining certificates for working with [Apple Pay](https://apple.com/apple-pay) and [Google Pay](https://pay.google.com).
- Launch of non-custodial cards with issuer [Wallester](https://wallester.com) and provider [Kauri.finance](http://Kauri.finance) (issuer [Quicko](https://quicko.com))
- Adding support for [Solana](https://solana.com) blockchain.

**2025**

- Connecting [Changelly](https://changelly.com) for topping up with all popular stablecoins.
- Reorganization of business team and business approach
- Creation of Trust smart contract
- Beginning of artificial intelligence implementation in the project.

# Security and Audit

The project underwent comprehensive security assessment, including audit of [Whales DMCC](https://whalesdmcc.com/) Holders smart contracts conducted by [Trail of Bits](https://trailofbits.com) in April 2025. A token evaluation policy was developed to ensure compliance with [MiCA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica) requirements and prevent work with anonymous assets.
