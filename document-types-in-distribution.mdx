---
title: "Document Types in Distribution"
---

| Document | Direction | When | Content |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Settlement Statement** | Issuer → <br />Merchant | Period close | Activated cheques count, <br />face value total, FX rate, <br />amount due in settlement currency |
| **Agent's Report** (future) | Merchant → <br />Issuer | With payment | Confirmation of sales, <br />amount remitted, <br />agent commission retained |
| **Rebate Notice** | Issuer → <br />Merchant | After settlement confirmed \+ <br />delay | Settled volume, <br />rebate rate applied, <br />rebate amount, payment method |
| **Platform Invoice** | Instance → <br />Merchant | Monthly | Platform service fees <br />(creation fee, etc.) — <br />separate from distribution settlement |

> **Critical distinction**: The **Settlement Statement** is a distribution document (face value settlement between issuer and agent). The **Platform Invoice** is a service fee document (infrastructure costs charged by the platform operator). These are different documents, different legal relationships, different amounts.
