## Invoice Denomination

Invoices denominated in **USDC** (~\$1.00 USD, Circle-managed peg).

Recommended columns (partially implemented):

```text
subtotal_usdc   NUMERIC(18,6)   -- primary
subtotal_usd    NUMERIC(18,6)   -- USD equivalent (planned)
rate_at_invoice NUMERIC(12,6)   -- USD/USDC at issuance (planned)
rate_source     TEXT            -- 'circle_api' | 'assumed_peg' (planned)
```

## Settlement Documents in Non-USD Currencies

Settlement statements (§2.5) involve FX conversion when the merchant operates in a non-USD currency. Store:

```text
face_value_usdc     NUMERIC(18,6)   -- always in USDC
settlement_currency TEXT            -- e.g. 'EUR'
fx_rate             NUMERIC(12,6)   -- USDC → settlement_currency at settlement date
fx_rate_source      TEXT            -- 'ecb', 'coinbase', 'manual'
amount_due          NUMERIC(18,6)   -- face_value_usdc × fx_rate, in settlement_currency
```

This is the authoritative amount the merchant owes. The FX rate is fixed at the time the settlement statement is **issued**, not at payment time — protecting both parties from rate drift during the payment window.

## Revenue Recognition

Under IRS guidance (Notice 2014-21, Rev. Rul. 2023-14):

- USDC received = ordinary income at FMV at receipt time
- FMV for USDC ≈ \$1.0000 (trivial, but must be documented)
- Store `usdc_rate_at_collection` and `usd_fmv` on every `fee_ledger` collected entry (planned)\

## On-Chain Payment Proof

`fee_ledger.collection_tx_hash` and `merchant_settlements.payment_ref` (Solana tx sig for crypto payments) are the immutable receipts. Verifiable at [solscan.io](http://solscan.io).
