## Adding a card through In-app provisioning

1. When creating a new card or navigating to a previously issued one, a button for adding the card to Google Pay is displayed.

   ## Manual Card Addition

   A payment card can be added to GPay/Google Wallet regardless of whether the application in which it is issued has undergone Google certification. It is sufficient for the issuing bank to have passed the appropriate certification.
