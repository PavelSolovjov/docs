# Card issuance

To issue a new card, go to Cards mode and use one of the following methods:

- Click on the card silhouette with the + image on the account card for which you want to issue a card, and follow the instructions.

<img
  src="/images/2026-02-2414.01.24.jpg"
  alt="2026 02 2414 01 24"
  title="2026 02 2414 01 24"
  className="mx-auto"
  style={{ width:"35%" }}
/>

- Click on the account card for which you want to issue a card, and follow the instructions.

# Hiding accounts

An account can be temporarily hidden from the list in Cards mode if it is not being used. To do this, swipe left on the desired account card and click on the icon with the crossed-out eye.

<img
  src="/images/2026-02-2414.01.27.jpg"
  alt="2026 02 2414 01 27"
  title="2026 02 2414 01 27"
  className="mx-auto"
  style={{ width:"35%" }}
/>

To restore the account display, go to the bottom of the interface and click the "Show" button. Then perform a left swipe on the hidden account and click on the eye icon — the account will return to the active list.
