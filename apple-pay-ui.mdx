## Adding a Card to Apple Pay Manually

A payment card can be added to [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/)/[Apple Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-wallet/id1160481993) regardless of whether the application in which it is issued has passed Apple certification. It is sufficient for the issuing bank to have passed the appropriate certification.\
**Card addition procedure:**

1. The user opens the [Apple Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-wallet/id1160481993) application

## Removing a Card from Apple Pay

Payment cards in Apple Pay can be added and removed at the user's discretion. When a card is removed from Apple Wallet, the ability to use it for contactless payments is lost. The card removal procedure is performed through the card management menu, located in the upper right corner of the application screen. It is important to note that removing a card from Apple Pay does not affect the processing of refund payments that arrive after its removal, since the issuing bank processes these transactions using the original payment card details.
