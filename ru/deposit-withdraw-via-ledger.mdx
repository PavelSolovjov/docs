title: "Deposit/withdraw via Ledger"
---

You can now stake your TON tokens directly through Ledger Live via the integrated [P2P.org](http://P2P.org) dApp. This allows you to earn rewards on your assets while maintaining full control and security using your Ledger device.

## Before you start

- Download and install the latest version of [Ledger Live](https://www.ledger.com/ledger-live) onto your computer or mobile device.
- Open Ledger Live, connect your Ledger device to the My Ledger section, and ensure your device is running the latest OS (firmware) version. Learn more [here](https://support.ledger.com/article/8458939792669-zd).
- Ensure the latest version of the Toncoin (TON) app is installed on your Ledger device.

## How to stake TON via Ledger Live

1. Open the Ledger Live app.
2. Click the Earn (%) icon in the left-hand menu.
3. View your options:
   - Assets earning rewards: Track rewards on assets you’re already staking.
   - Earn more rewards: See assets available for staking and estimated yields.
4. Click Earn more rewards.

## Withdrawing your TON back to your available TON balance

You can track the status of your stake or withdraw it at any time you prefer. Follow the steps below:

1. On the Earn dashboard, next to your staked position, find the three dots and click **Manage** to open the [P2P.org](http://P2P.org) tab.
2. Go to the **Positions** tab on the [P2P.org](http://P2P.org) tab in Ledger Live to view your active TON stake.
3. Check staking details, pending rewards, and withdrawal options.
