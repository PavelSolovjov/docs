# Что такое Apple Pay

[Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/) — это мобильный платежный сервис и цифровой кошелек [Apple Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-wallet/id1160481993), разработанный [Apple Inc](https://www.apple.com/). Этот сервис позволяет выполнять бесконтактные платежи через устройства Apple, включая iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch и Mac.

# Возможные ограничения для пользователя

1. **Совместимые устройства**: [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/) доступен только на устройствах [Apple](https://www.apple.com/), таких как [iPhone](https://www.apple.com/iphone/), [iPad](https://www.apple.com/ipad/), [Apple Watch](https://www.apple.com/watch/) и [Mac](https://www.apple.com/mac/), которые поддерживают эту функцию.
2. **Поддерживаемые банки и карты**: не все финансовые организации и платежные карты совместимы с [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/). При заключении договора с банком-эмитентом бизнес-команда проверяет наличие поддержки этой технологии у конкретного банка.
3. **Региональные ограничения**: доступность [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/) зависит от географического региона. [Список поддерживаемых регионов](https://support.apple.com/ru-ru/102775).
4. **Лимиты транзакций**: банки-эмитенты могут устанавливать ограничения на максимальную сумму транзакций, проводимых через [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/).
5. **Кешбэк и программы лояльности**: отдельные эмитенты могут не предоставлять кешбэк или бонусы при оплате через [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/).
6. **Количество карт**: максимальное число карт, доступных пользователю в [Apple Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-wallet/id1160481993), равно 12, но это значение может меняться, например, при привязке нескольких аккаунтов в приложении.

# In-app provisioning

Holders реализует продвинутую интеграцию с [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/) через технологию [In-app provisioning](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/passkit/apple_pay/setting_up_apple_pay). Эта технология позволяет добавлять платежную карту в [Apple Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-wallet/id1160481993) напрямую из приложения на платформе [iOS](https://www.apple.com/ios/), что значительно упрощает процедуру подключения карты для пользователя и устраняет необходимость переходить в настройки [Apple Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-wallet/id1160481993).\
Чтобы использовать технологию [In-app provisioning](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/passkit/apple_pay/setting_up_apple_pay), организация должна пройти обязательную сертификацию [Apple](https://www.apple.com/). [Apple](https://www.apple.com/) делегирует право сертификации аккредитованным организациям, таким как [Fime](https://www.fime.com/).\
Наличие [In-app provisioning](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/passkit/apple_pay/setting_up_apple_pay) не отменяет возможности ручного добавления карты в [Apple Wallet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-wallet/id1160481993) через интерфейс самого кошелька.

# Сертификация In-app provisioning

To implement [in-app provisioning](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/passkit/apple_pay/setting_up_apple_pay) functionality, a company must establish partnership relationships with each issuing bank whose cards are planned to be integrated into the system. Each financial institution undergoes an individual technical verification and certification procedure to confirm full compatibility of banking systems with [in-app provisioning](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/passkit/apple_pay/setting_up_apple_pay) technology and the [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/) payment ecosystem.\
**The certification process includes the following stages:**

1. Submitting a certification application.
2. Signing a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) with the certifying organization.
3. Signing a contract and paying for certification services.
4. Agreeing on certification timeline. The testing procedure is carried out by an accredited company specialist within 5 business days.
5. Providing marketing materials to the certifying organization:
   1. [Apple Pay page on the application website](https://tonhub.com/apple-pay)
   2. [Application Video](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/passkit/apple_pay/setting_up_apple_pay) - demonstration video material of the provisioning process
   3. [Marketing Creatives](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/) - social media materials about [In-app provisioning](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/passkit/apple_pay/setting_up_apple_pay) implementation (in the absence of a large-scale launch, a [Splash screen](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/launch-screen) is sufficient)

Testing is conducted on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch devices.\
Upon completion of testing:

1. The accredited organization representative prepares a report for Apple, including integration and testing checklists.
2. Apple reviews the certification results within 2 weeks from submission and assigns a launch date by which the feature must be removed from whitelists and ready for full operation.

# Верификация карты пользователем

After adding a card to Apple Wallet, Apple performs a verification procedure to confirm the user's ownership rights to the given payment card. In some cases, verification is performed automatically, however the standard procedure involves verification through a confirmation code that is sent via SMS message to the phone number specified by the user when registering their profile in the Holders system.

<Info>
  **Important feature:** Apple Pay does not transmit the actual credit or debit card number when making transactions. Instead, the system generates a unique digital token that replaces the user's real card data. Each transaction is accompanied by the creation of an individual dynamic security code, which prevents the possibility of token reuse in case of compromise. This architecture ensures that payment documents display an alternative card number instead of the original one.
</Info>

## Статусы верификации карты в Apple

**Green** - the card passes verification immediately without additional requests.\
**Yellow status** — to complete the verification procedure, the user needs to enter a one-time confirmation code (OTP).\
**Orange status** — the card is subject to verification exclusively in manual mode through the partner bank.\
Red - the card cannot be verified

## Ответственность за передачу карты

Section 5.3.11 of the [Wallester Payment Service Agreement](https://wallester.com/private-account-card-agreement) states the following:

> "The Card must only be used by the Client. The Client must not authorise third parties to use the card, i.e., to make the Card or only the Card Data available to the third parties. The card must not be given to third parties as a gift, sold to third parties or otherwise transferred to third parties."

This means that the card must be used only by the client, and the client has no right to authorize third parties to use the card, transfer it, give it as a gift, or sell it.\
Accordingly, any actions involving the transfer of the card to other persons or granting them the right to use the card are qualified as a violation of the terms of the agreement with Wallester. Such violations result in the application of sanctions in the form of card blocking. In certain cases, depending on the nature of the use of card data and the intent of the participants, such actions may be qualified as fraudulent, which provides for criminal liability in the form of monetary penalties or imprisonment.

# Внешний вид карты

Users cannot independently change the card design in Apple Wallet. The card design in Apple Wallet is set by the bank or financial institution that issued the card. Each design is approved by the issuer with the payment system and added to the card settings after approval. A similar situation occurs with the bank name that is displayed on the card in Apple Wallet. Holders can change or add designs, but each change will require VISA approval and additional costs.\
**Cost of adding a design:** 400 EUR\
**Cost of a minor change:** 100 EUR\
A minor change is considered, for example, changing a phone number or logo on the card.\
Adding a design to Apple Wallet happens automatically and does not require approvals or additional payments.
