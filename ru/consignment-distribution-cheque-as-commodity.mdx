---
title: "Consignment Distribution — Cheque as Commodity"
---

A USDC cheque is not just a digital instrument — in the distribution model it is a product with a face value that acts as its price tag. The issuer manufactures batches, and merchants are distributors who sell these products to end customers.

![Scheme21](/images/Scheme21.png)

## Key Financial Events

| Event | Effect on Merchant Debt |
| :-- | :-- |
| Batch shipped to merchant | Contingent liability created for cheques in stock |
| Cheque activated (sold) | Liability fixed and increased by the face value |
| Cheque returned (unsold) | Liability reduced by the face value |
| Cheque expired unactivated | Returned to issuer and liability cancelled |
| Settlement payment received | Debt cleared by the paid amount |
| Rebate paid by issuer | Separate credit to merchant and not a debt reduction |
