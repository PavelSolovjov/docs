---
title: "Issuing account statement of funds movement"
---

To obtain an account statement, you need to perform the following actions:

1. In the account settings, select the "Statements" section
2. In the opened menu, click "Account Statements"

<img
  src="/images/download(12).png"
  alt="Download(12)"
  title="Download(12)"
  className="mx-auto"
  style={{ width:"48%" }}
/>

3. Specify the account for which you need to obtain a statement

<img
  src="/images/download(32).jpeg"
  alt="Download(32)"
  title="Download(32)"
  className="mx-auto"
  style={{ width:"29%" }}
/>

4. Select the statement generation period (you can specify a custom period or use preset intervals)

<img
  src="/images/download(33).jpeg"
  alt="Download(33)"
  title="Download(33)"
  className="mx-auto"
  style={{ width:"28%" }}
/>

5. After submitting the request, the system will generate the statement and send it to the email address linked to your profile. The statement generation time is up to 15 minutes.
