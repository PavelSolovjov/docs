# Deposit (sending funds to staking)

Deposit to the liquid pool is carried out in two ways:

- **Buying tokens on DEX.** Users can purchase wsTON on a decentralized exchange by following [this link](https://app.dedust.io/swap/TON/wsTON) and making a swap in the TON/wsTON trading pair. This method is the simplest, however when exchanging significant amounts of TON, the rate will be less favorable compared to direct staking. For example, when exchanging 1,000 TON through DEX, a user will receive 917.04 wsTON, while when sending to staking — 926.4 wsTON.
- Sending TON to staking through the Tonhub interface or [tonwhales.com](http://tonwhales.com) website. This method is more profitable than the first one and we will examine it in more detail.

## Deposit through [tonwhales.com](http://tonwhales.com) website

1. Go to https://tonwhales.com/staking/liquid and click the "Stake" button.
2. In the opened dialog box, perform the following actions:

- Specify the amount in TON that needs to be sent to staking.

# Withdrawing funds from liquid staking

Withdrawing funds from the liquid pool is carried out in two ways:

- **Selling tokens on DEX.** Users can sell wsTON on a decentralized exchange by following [this link](https://app.dedust.io/swap/TON/wsTON) and making a swap in the TON/wsTON trading pair. This method is the simplest, however when exchanging significant amounts of TON, the rate will be less favorable compared to withdrawing from staking. For example, when exchanging 1,000 wsTON through DEX, a user will receive 1,065.8 TON, whereas when withdrawing from staking — 1,079 TON.
- Withdrawing TON from staking through the Tonhub interface or [tonwhales.com](http://tonwhales.com) website. This method is more profitable than the first and we will examine it in more detail.

## Withdrawing funds through the [tonwhales.com](http://tonwhales.com) website interface

1. Go to the page https://tonwhales.com/staking/liquid and click the "Withdraw" button.
2. Select the withdrawal method:

- Connect your Tonhub wallet by scanning the QR code (connection is made through the [Tonconnect](https://github.com/ton-connect) protocol). Confirm the transaction that appears. After the cycle completes, repeat the withdrawal request — TON will arrive in your balance within a few minutes.
- Click "Manual withdraw" to perform a manual withdrawal.
  - Send a transaction to the pool address with the comment "Withdraw". You will need to pay a 0.2 TON fee to submit the request. After the cycle completes, send another transaction to the pool address with the comment "Collect". You will also need to pay a 0.2 TON fee to submit this request. TON will arrive in your balance within a few minutes.

## Withdrawing funds through the Tonhub interface

1. Go to the staking section by clicking on the banner on the main screen.
2. Select the Whales Liquid pool and click Withdraw.
3. Specify the amount to withdraw from staking. To submit the request, you will need to pay a fee of 0.2 TON.

<img
  src="/images/2025-12-2914.57.55-1.jpg"

  title="2025 12 2914 57 55 1"
  className="mx-auto"

/>

4. Upon completion of the cycle, funds ready for withdrawal will be displayed on the pool page. You need to press the withdrawal button again. To submit the second request, you will need to pay a commission of 0.2 TON. The funds will arrive at the specified address within a few minutes.
