Whales AG is a Swiss company that creates an international system for crypto payments through a managed network of subsidiary companies. This network includes regional VASPs (Virtual Asset Service Providers) that operate under local laws in countries with clear crypto legislation, as well as separate companies for development (Software House) and technology rights management (IP Box). Whales AG manages the entire group of companies, attracts major investors, and builds long-term relationships with shareholders based on complete transparency and Swiss reliability.

# Fundamental Values

**Honesty, Openness, Transparency** in all aspects of operations.

# Whales Mission

## Give everyone the right to a global account in a stable currency

We sincerely believe that in the conditions of global capitalism, every person, regardless of citizenship and place of residence, should have access to a payment account and stable currency for life and business.

# Whales Vision

**Creating a global network of subsidiary companies** under the umbrella of Swiss holding company **Whales AG**.\
**The network structure includes:**

- **Regional VASPs** (Virtual Asset Service Providers): Companies established for legal operational activities in specific countries and to ensure clarity for local regulators.
- **Software House:** Development center and operational team located in a convenient jurisdiction for optimizing workflows and taxation.
- **IP Box:** Structure for managing intellectual property (software) and further tax optimization.
- **Experimental Jurisdiction:** A dedicated company in a flexible jurisdiction for safe testing of hypotheses and innovations.

**Fundamental principle:** Complete transparency and openness of business. We are not anonymous: management (CEO) and key specialists are known and accessible.\
**We build our business honestly, without deception or secrecy.**
