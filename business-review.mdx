Whales AG is a Swiss company that creates an international system for crypto payments through a managed network of subsidiary companies. This network includes regional VASPs (Virtual Asset Service Providers) that operate under local laws in countries with clear crypto legislation, as well as separate companies for development (Software House) and technology rights management (IP Box). Whales AG manages the entire group of companies, attracts major investors, and builds long-term relationships with shareholders based on complete transparency and Swiss reliability.

# Fundamental Values

**Honesty, Openness, Transparency** in all aspects of operations.

# Whales Mission

## Give everyone the right to a global account in a stable currency

We sincerely believe that in the conditions of global capitalism, every person, regardless of citizenship and place of residence, should have access to a payment account and stable currency for life and business.

# Whales Vision

**Creating a global network of subsidiary companies** under the umbrella of Swiss holding company **Whales AG**.\
**The network structure includes:**

- **Regional VASPs** (Virtual Asset Service Providers): Companies established for legal operational activities in specific countries and to ensure clarity for local regulators.
- **Software House:** Development center and operational team located in a convenient jurisdiction for optimizing workflows and taxation.
- **IP Box:** Structure for managing intellectual property (software) and further tax optimization.
- **Experimental Jurisdiction:** A dedicated company in a flexible jurisdiction for safe testing of hypotheses and innovations.

**Fundamental principle:** Complete transparency and openness of business. We are not anonymous: management (CEO) and key specialists are known and accessible.\
**We build our business honestly, without deception or secrecy.**

# History

**2021**

- The team first learned about the [TON (The Open Network)](https://ton.org) blockchain, originally developed by Telegram as a scalable decentralized platform
- Created a miner that first enabled efficient mining of TON tokens using graphics processing units (GPUs)
- Founded the world's first and largest mining pool for the TON network, laying the foundation for further ecosystem and holding development

**2022**

- Launched our own cryptocurrency wallet [Tonhub](https://gettonhub.com), which became an important tool for user interaction with the TON blockchain
- Developed and implemented [the world's first smart contract for TON token staking](https://github.com/tonwhales/ton-nominators)
- Created [the first staking pool](https://tonwhales.com/staking) in the ecosystem, allowing users to earn passive income from holding TON tokens

**2023**

- Active work on the [Holders](https://holders.io) project began
- Developed the first version of [a smart contract on the TON blockchain](/holders/product-review/accounts/ton-and-usdt-smart-contracts) for issuing and managing bank cards
- Submitted necessary documents to the international payment system [VISA](https://visa.com) to obtain cryptographic approval (crypto approval)
- Negotiations began with the first strategic partners to organize KYC and card issuance

**2024**

- Received official permission from [VISA](https://visa.com) to work with crypto assets
- Implemented [a decentralized accounts system](/holders/product-review/accounts-and-smart-contracts/Accountssmart-contractsoverview), providing users with full control over their funds
- Signed a strategic agreement with fintech company [Wallester](https://wallester.com) to issue the first non-custodial bank cards for European users
- Conducted deep integration of the Holders platform with the [Tonhub](https://gettonhub.com) wallet, creating a unified ecosystem for crypto asset management
- Developed and implemented [a contract to support payments in USDT](/holders/product-review/accounts/ton-and-usdt-smart-contracts) stablecoin on the TON network
- Concluded an agreement with [Triple A](https://triple-a.io) to provide users with the ability to make traditional [SEPA and SWIFT](/holders/product-review/accounts/account-operations) bank transfers from their crypto accounts
- Obtained [Apple Pay](https://apple.com/apple-pay) and [Google Pay](https://pay.google.com) certification for direct card addition to mobile wallets
- Expanded cryptocurrency support: added USDC on the [Solana](https://solana.com) network to the Holders platform and SOL support in Tonhub
- Began development of the @card Telegram bot to simplify card management through the Telegram messenger
- Opened the first partner staking pools for popular [Tonkeeper](https://tonkeeper.com) wallets and the ePN platform

**2025**

- Added the ability to fund accounts through a wide range of popular blockchains thanks to partnership with the [Changelly](https://changelly.com) exchange service
- Developed a specialized [smart contract for creating vesting accounts](/holders/product-review/trust-and-vesting) that ensure gradual release of locked tokens
- Began implementing artificial intelligence in the Holders platform user interface to improve user experience
- Conducted a radical restructuring of the business approach and complete renewal of the business team
- Applied for a [MICA (Markets in Crypto-Assets)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32023R1114) license - new European regulatory standards for crypto assets
- Integrated the [Changelly](https://changelly.com) service directly into the Tonhub wallet for direct cryptocurrency exchange
- Implemented the innovative Post Cash service for creating stablecoin checks
- Concluded agreements and launched staking pools for major institutional partners [Ledger](https://ledger.com) and [P2P](https://p2p.org)
